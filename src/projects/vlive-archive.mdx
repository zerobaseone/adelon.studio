Archiving and preserving K-pop media from defunct platforms like VLIVE before they disappear forever. Digital preservation work focused on maintaining access to cultural content.

## The Challenge

When VLIVE announced its shutdown, thousands of hours of fan content were at risk of being lost permanently.

## My Approach

Systematically downloading and organizing content with proper metadata to ensure long-term preservation and accessibility.
